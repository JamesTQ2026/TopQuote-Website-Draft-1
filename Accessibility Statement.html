Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making the TopQuote website usable for everyone, and how to get help.

Draft, pending legal & compliance review. This wording is a working draft for the pre-launch mockup and must be reviewed and approved (including against The Openwork Partnership's compliance-approved templates) before publication.

We want this website to be usable by as many people as possible and are working towards the WCAG 2.2 AA standard.

What we do

  • Use clear, plain-English content and a readable type hierarchy.
  • Provide text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Mute background video by default and aim to provide controls.
  • Support keyboard navigation and reasonable colour contrast.

Known limitations

Some interactive and embedded elements are still being reviewed for full accessibility. We are actively improving these.

Getting help or reporting a problem

If you have difficulty using any part of this site, or need information in a different format, contact us at hello@topquote.uk.com or 0161 974 3710 and we will help.
