What is Kids Pass and Popcorn Pass?

When you take out a life/CIC policy with TopQuote you’ll qualify for 12 months free access to Kids Pass or Popcorn Pass, the choice is yours!

Kids Pass is the UK’s leading family days out and discount membership. You can save on everything from holidays, theme parks, eating out, going to the cinema, or even visiting the zoo.

Popcorn Pass offers up to 40% off the UK’s major cinema retailers, whilst also offering up to 50% off some of the highstreets best restaurants.

Here’s how to access Kids Pass & Popcorn Pass

1

Purchase a life insurance/CIC policy from TopQuote.

2

Visit www.kidspass.co.uk or www.popcornpass.co.uk.

3

Input the unique serial number (which we’ll send to you when your policy goes live) and your details onto the website above.

4

Once you have successfully completed the registration process, an activation email will be sent to the email address entered on the website confirming your 12-month membership!

NOTE: Please ensure you check your junk/spam folders to ensure that you are in receipt of all relevant communications. It is your responsibility to do so. *Policy premiums must be maintained for the entirety of the offer. Full details, terms and conditions are available on request.
