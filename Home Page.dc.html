Cover Types

Level Term Insurance

Your cover amount and monthly payments stay the same for the whole policy, the payout is fixed, ideal for ongoing commitments like family income or an interest-only mortgage.

Learn More

Decreasing Term Insurance

Your cover amount reduces over time, usually in line with a repayment mortgage, while monthly payments stay the same. Often the most affordable way to protect a shrinking debt.
