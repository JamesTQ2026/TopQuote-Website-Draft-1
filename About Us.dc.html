Our story

Two decades of putting
our customers first

TopQuote is a UK life insurance and protection broker, based in Cheshire and serving customers across the country. We've spent over twenty years arranging life insurance, critical illness cover and income protection for families and individuals, working with leading providers like Legal & General. We're FCA-authorised through The Openwork Partnership.

We were founded on a simple belief: people deserve more than a policy number. They deserve an adviser who listens, understands their circumstances, and stays in touch as life changes, not just when there's something to sell. Whether that means moving house, having a child, or starting a new job, your protection should keep up.

Every customer has a dedicated TopQuote adviser. That means you'll always speak to someone who knows your situation, not just your policy number, and the same person, every call.

Recognised on The
Christie's Supporter Wall

We're proud to share that TopQuote has been recognised on The Christie Charity's new Recognition Wall, marking our continued support for the charity since 2021.

The Christie, based in Manchester, is one of Europe's leading cancer centres and a vital lifeline for patients across the Northwest. The Recognition Wall celebrates the supporters whose contributions help make the charity's life-saving work possible.

We were honoured to receive a beautiful recognition medallion this week, a replica of the one originally presented when TopQuote first contributed in 2021. It now sits proudly in our Cheshire office as a reminder of why supporting causes like this matters.

Charitable giving has always been part of how we work at TopQuote. We're grateful to be a small part of The Christie's story.
