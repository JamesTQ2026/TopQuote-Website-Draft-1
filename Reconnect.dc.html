Reconnect

What could be more important than the people you love?

If your original broker or adviser is no longer looking after your policy, you're not alone, and you're not stuck. Reconnect is our way of picking up where they left off, making sure the cover you took out to protect your family is still doing its job.

We're now looking after
your Legal & General policy

Your Legal & General life or critical illness cover is unchanged, your premiums, benefits and policy terms all stay exactly as they are. The only thing that's different is who you'll hear from when it's time for a review or an update. That's now us.

Why the change?

Your previous broker no longer works with Legal & General, so Legal & General have appointed TopQuote to take over the servicing of policies like yours.

What we'll do for you

Keep your contact details current, make sure you're aware of every benefit you're entitled to, check nothing's been missed when it comes to claims, and review your cover whenever your circumstances change.

You don't need to do anything right now. If you have questions about your policy, speak to your TopQuote adviser.
