This policy explains how this website uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can manage your choices.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Some are essential for the website to work; others help us understand how the site is used or enable embedded content.

Categories we use

Strictly necessary

Required for the site to function and for your cookie preferences to be remembered. These do not require consent.

Analytics

Help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. Set only with your consent.

Embedded content (maps, video, social)

Pages that embed Google Maps, video or social links may set third-party cookies. These are loaded only with your consent.

Managing cookies

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies using the banner shown when you first visit, and change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data. You can also control cookies through your browser settings.
