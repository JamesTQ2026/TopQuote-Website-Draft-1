Who are Vitality?

Vitality is a UK company offering health insurance and life insurance. Its approach centres on the Vitality Programme, an incentive-based health promotion programme.[1]

Vitality also runs the Vitality Research Institute, which studies the relationship between behaviours and health outcomes.[1]

ProgrammeThe Vitality Programme rewards members for healthier choices
Shared ValueHealthier members, the insurer and society benefit from the same behaviours
