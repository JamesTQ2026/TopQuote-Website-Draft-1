Why It Matters

What would your family do without your income?

Life insurance isn't morbid, it's responsible. Here's what a payout could cover for your family.

Keep a Roof Over Their Heads

Rent or mortgage payments don't stop. A lump sum payout means your family can stay in the home they know without financial pressure.

Clear Outstanding Debts

Credit cards, loans, and other debts don't disappear. Life insurance ensures your family inherits security, not your liabilities.

Serious Illness Cover

Critical Illness Cover pays a lump sum if you're diagnosed with cancer, heart attack, or stroke, giving you time to recover without financial worry.

Replace Your Income

Especially critical if you're self-employed or your partner relies on your salary. Income Protection can bridge the gap while you recover or in the event of serious illness.

Products

Cover Options For Your Family

TopQuote advisers will help you choose the right combination for your family's situation.

Level Term Life Insurance

A fixed lump sum paid to your family if you pass away during the policy term. The payout stays the same throughout, ideal for families with ongoing costs.

Decreasing Term Life Insurance

Cover that reduces in line with an outstanding debt over time. A cost-effective way to ensure debts don't fall to your family.

Critical Illness Cover

Pays a tax-free lump sum if you're diagnosed with a serious illness like cancer, heart attack, or stroke, while you're still alive.

Income Protection

Pays a regular monthly income if illness or injury stops you working, helping your family keep up with everyday costs while you recover.
