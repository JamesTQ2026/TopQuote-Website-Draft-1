Consumer Duty In Action

The Reconnect programme

Reconnect is how we make ongoing support real, not theoretical. It's our structured engagement programme for protection customers whose original broker is no longer servicing them. Rather than waiting for the customer to come to us, we proactively make contact, review whether the cover still fits their life, update key information, and flag anything that's drifted out of date.

It's the difference between a policy that quietly ages and a policy that keeps doing its job. We've been running it for over a decade, and it remains one of the clearest examples of what Consumer Duty looks like when it's lived rather than written down.
